One EspoCRM for requests, responses and sites, fed by KoboToolbox

A KoboToolbox form submission becomes a case, a volunteer's profile or a site in EspoCRM, in the right team, with the right people notified. Responses get their own task board, open to their own team only. And lists kept in EspoCRM, such as sites and standards, come back to the Kobo forms as choices. One instance, with access kept apart by teams and roles. This was tested end to end, with made-up data.

  1. Someone fills in a Kobo form

  2. The bridge brings it to EspoCRM

    Within about 30 seconds: a case, a contact or a site, with the photos, in one team, and a notification for whoever should act.

  3. EspoCRM sends lists back

    The response's sites, the standards: as choices on the Kobo forms, kept up to date by themselves.

What it is

Three pieces:

  • KoboToolbox, unchanged: the forms people already use, on the web or in KoboCollect.
  • EspoCRM 10, open source, where staff, partners, roster experts and volunteers each see their own team's work. It has cases, contacts and accounts, and here also Responses, Sites and Standards, with a Kanban board for tasks.
  • kobo-bridge, a small program that connects them. It polls Kobo for new submissions and turns each one into a record. It keeps lists from EspoCRM on Kobo forms. And it applies follow-up visits to the records they're about. It was taken from Kobo in a box's case management and made general, so this test, the box and later connections share one tested bridge.

Nothing changes in KoboToolbox's settings: no REST services or webhooks to set up. After either side has been down, the bridge catches up by itself, and never makes a record twice.

VolunteersVolunteerAs roster expertsMove their own tasks and comment; no creating or reassigning

Volunteers and roster experts are ordinary EspoCRM users with restrictive roles, not portal users: EspoCRM doesn't let portal users be assigned work.

Three ways one team's work could reach another team turned up in testing. All three are closed now by EspoCRM's own formula scripts (no paid extensions):

  1. Assigning across teams. EspoCRM's "assign within the team" checks the teams of the person assigning, not the record's. A triager in two consortia could give a Consortium A case to a Consortium B partner, who would then see it. Now refused: the assignee, and anyone added to a task, must be in the record's team.
  2. Old assignees stay collaborators. EspoCRM 10 adds each assignee to a task's collaborators and keeps them there. Collaborators can read a task whatever their teams. Now a reassigned task drops its previous assignee.
  3. Leaving a team doesn't hide your own tasks. People still see the tasks assigned to them after they leave the team. Closing a response now gives all its tasks back to its lead.

One instance or several?

One instance works for both workflows, on these conditions:

  • the roles and formula scripts as tested here are kept (they're in the repository, and the seed puts them back);
  • non-staff roles never get "all" access to anything but the shared standards;
  • someone in several teams sees all of them: for a partner in two consortia, that's by design;
  • when someone leaves a response, their open tasks are reassigned (closing the response does it for all).

What stays shared across everything: administrators, fields, layouts and automations are instance-wide, and an administrator sees all. That's the main reason to choose a separate instance: a partner who needs their own administrator, or data that must be kept on its own server.

Limits and open questions

  • Collaborators can read and comment, not edit (EspoCRM 10). A task with several people working on it is moved by its assignee.
  • No paid Advanced Pack. Notifications are mentions posted by the bridge. Starter tasks and the team rules are formula scripts. Escalation of unassigned cases is done by the bridge. The test instance sends no email (no SMTP), so notifications appear in the app only.
  • Saved board filters are per person: EspoCRM has no shared saved filter without custom code. A response's page lists its tasks and sites, and its members see only its board anyway.
  • Lists go to every phone with the form. Only the columns a template names are included; keep personal data out of them.
  • Sign-in with Google or Microsoft (through an OIDC bridge such as authentik) wasn't tested; it's the plan's optional last phase.

Open questions are in the repository's REPORT.md.
