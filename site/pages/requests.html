Requests from a Kobo form

Partners and staff ask for support with a KoboToolbox form. Each submission becomes a case in EspoCRM for its project's team, linked to whoever sent it and their organisation, with their files attached. The project's triager is notified and passes it to someone to work on. Each consortium sees its own cases and no one else's.

The form

Kobo support request (kobo/forms/support-request.json, also as .xlsx) asks for:

  • the requester's name, email and organisation;
  • the project: Kobo internal, Consortium A or Consortium B;
  • a category, how urgent it is, a summary and a description;
  • optionally, a file.

Anyone with the form's link can use it, in a browser or in KoboCollect. They need no EspoCRM account.

A request becomes a case

Within about 30 seconds (4 to 26 s in testing) the bridge opens a case:

On the caseFrom
Name: "Data and exports: Export to XLS times out"Category and summary
Priority (Low … Urgent), category, projectThe answers
Team: one, the project'sThe project answer ([[route]] in the template)
ContactThe requester, found by email or created; a second request from them reuses it
AccountThe organisation, found by name or created
AttachmentsThe submitted file, copied from Kobo, so it opens without a Kobo account
Source "Kobo form", and a Kobo panel: project, submission, linkThe bridge

A case is in one team. EspoCRM would allow several, so EspoCRM refuses a second one when anyone tries to add it ("A case can be in one team only").

Triage

The triager gets a notification for each new case of their projects. They open it and assign it to someone in the case's team. That person is notified in turn.

Someone can only be given a case if they're in its team. EspoCRM on its own would let a triager who works for two consortia hand a Consortium A case to a Consortium B partner, who would then see it. So EspoCRM is set to refuse that ("The assignee is not in the case's team").

Nobody picked it up

A case that is still unassigned some time after it came in ([escalation] after_hours: one minute on the test instance, a few hours for real) gets a note mentioning the team's lead, once. Closed or assigned cases are left alone. EspoCRM's own workflows could do this too, but they're in the paid Advanced Pack, so the bridge does it.

Consortia and partners

Each project or consortium is a team: Project: Consortium A (Kobo and three organisations), Project: Consortium B (Kobo and five). A consortium's partner staff are users with the role Partner Staff in its team:

The same goes for the requesters and organisations (contacts and accounts), which take the case's team. Partners can't see the other consortium's records in lists, in search or by link, and can't move a case into the other team. Adding a consortium is adding a team, a route in the template, and its people (see Running it).

A partner who submits requests but shouldn't log in needs nothing in EspoCRM: they just use the form. EspoCRM's portal (for external users who follow their own requests) wasn't part of this test.

When something goes wrong

  • A submission missing an answer the template requires, or one EspoCRM refuses (an invalid email, say), becomes no case. Nothing half-made is left behind: no stray contact or account. It waits in the bridge's ledger with the reason, and is retried after 1 minute, 10 minutes, 1 hour, then every 6 hours. The submissions after it carry on. bridge.py failed <state dir> lists what's waiting and why.
  • EspoCRM or Kobo down: the bridge's pass fails, and its status says why. When they're back, the next pass catches up, with no duplicates (tested by stopping EspoCRM mid-test).
  • The same submission seen twice (a lost ledger, a restart) never makes a second case. The bridge looks for the submission's uuid first.
